# Captain Orlov

A veteran cloud-runner of forty winters, Captain Orlov has survived seven dives into the Boiling Shelf. Cynical, precise, and ferociously protective of his crew, he treats the *Cloud-Wisp* as an extension of his own scarred flesh.
