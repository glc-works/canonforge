# Lyra Valen

Lyra is a nineteen-year-old high-altitude rigger aboard the salvage skiff *Cloud-Wisp*. Raised on the lower catwalks of Dock Forty, she possesses an uncanny sensitivity to atmospheric pressure drops and aether shear currents.
